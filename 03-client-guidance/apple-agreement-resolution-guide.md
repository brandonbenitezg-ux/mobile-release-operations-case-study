# Client Guide: Resolving an Updated Apple Developer Agreement

## Purpose

Use this guide when an updated Apple Developer Program agreement is preventing or may delay an app submission. The Account Holder must complete the legal acceptance step; a Mobile App Operations Associate can identify the item, explain the steps, track completion, verify the status, and escalate when necessary.

## Simulation Notice

This guide is part of a fictional portfolio work sample. It was prepared from public Apple documentation without access to a real client developer account. Interface wording may change, so users should follow the current instructions displayed by Apple.

## Before You Begin

Confirm the following:

- You are the Apple Developer Program Account Holder for the organization.
- You can sign in to the Apple Account connected to the developer membership.
- Two-factor authentication is available.
- You are authorized by your organization to accept legal agreements.

If you are not the Account Holder, stop and forward this guide to that person. Do not share passwords or two-factor authentication codes with the operations team.

## Resolution Steps

1. Open [Apple Developer Account](https://developer.apple.com/account/).
2. Sign in using the Apple Account associated with the organization's developer membership.
3. Complete two-factor authentication when Apple requests it.
4. Look for an alert stating that the Apple Developer Program License Agreement has been updated.
5. Select the option to review the agreement.
6. Read the updated terms and confirm that you are authorized to accept them for the organization.
7. Select the displayed acceptance checkbox or confirmation control.
8. Select `Agree`, `Accept`, or the equivalent confirmation shown by Apple.
9. Refresh the account page.
10. Confirm that the updated-agreement alert no longer appears.
11. Sign out and sign back in if the alert remains after refreshing.
12. Notify the Mobile App Operations contact that the action is complete. Include the completion date and time, but do not send passwords, verification codes, legal documents, tax information, or banking information.

## Verification by Mobile App Operations

After the client reports completion, the operations owner should:

- Check whether the agreement warning or submission blocker has cleared.
- Record the verification date and current status.
- Update the tracking item from `Waiting on Client` to `Resolved`.
- Continue release preparation only after the blocker is confirmed as cleared.
- Preserve a redacted status screenshot if permitted by organizational policy.

## Troubleshooting

### The user cannot see the agreement alert

- Confirm that the user signed into the correct organizational developer account.
- Confirm that the user holds the Account Holder role.
- Sign out, sign back in, and check again.
- Review any notices displayed in both the Apple Developer account and App Store Connect.

### The user does not have Account Holder access

Identify the organization's current Account Holder and reassign the action. Other users should not attempt to accept an agreement on the Account Holder's behalf.

### The warning remains after acceptance

- Refresh the page.
- Sign out and sign back in.
- Allow a short period for the account status to update.
- Capture the exact warning text without exposing private account information.
- Escalate to Apple Developer Support if the blocker remains.

## Escalation Information

If escalation is required, collect:

- Organization name
- Team ID, if organizational policy permits sharing it with Apple Support
- Exact warning text
- Date and time the agreement was accepted
- Browser and operating system used
- Redacted screenshot of the remaining warning
- Actions already attempted

Never include a password or two-factor authentication code in an escalation message.
